Step 2 of 6
Which path are you exploring?
Your path determines everything — the timeline, the risks, the upside, and what matters most. Pick the one that resonates most right now, even if you're not 100% sure.
Cerulli Associates — U.S. Advisor Metrics 2025
"In 2025, an estimated 25,443 advisors managing $3.06 trillion in assets switched firms — with roughly two-fifths of those moves crossing channels entirely."
This isn't unusual. This is the market. The question is what your move looks like specifically.
🏛️
Path One
Wirehouse to Wirehouse
Lateral move to a competing wirehouse or regional firm. Familiar environment, significant transition package, fastest execution.
Transition packages currently ranging 200–350% of trailing 12-month production
Fastest timeline — 30–60 days from decision to move
Same regulatory environment — minimal compliance friction
Risk: trading one set of constraints for another
⚡
Path Two
Independent Broker-Dealer
Move to an IBD like LPL, Raymond James, Osaic, or Cetera. More freedom, higher payouts, still with BD infrastructure behind you.
Payouts typically 85–95% vs. 35–50% at wirehouses
LPL Financial: $619B AUM, +33% YoY growth — largest IBD in the US
Access to open-architecture platforms — Envestnet, Orion, AssetMark
Timeline: 60–90 days, moderate compliance transition
🚀
Path Three
RIA / Full Independence
Launch your own RIA or join an existing one. Maximum payout, full ownership, complete autonomy over your practice.
Independent RIAs: 5.2% CAGR headcount growth over 10 years (Cerulli)
RIA AUM growing at 11.2% annually — fastest of any channel
88% of independent RIAs say they're very likely to stay in channel
Fintrx tracks 40,000+ RIA entities — the market has never been bigger
