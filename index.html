How I help

Built for every stage
of your career.

From your first thought of leaving to your first day in a new chapter — every move is different, every advisor's situation is unique.

01
Firm transitions
Moving broker-dealers or joining an RIA? I help advisors evaluate their options, leverage deal structures, and make the right move — so your book stays intact and your clients stay confident.
02
Succession planning
Your practice is your legacy. Whether you're selling, finding a successor, or planning one more move before you exit — I help you structure it the right way and get the deal done on your terms.
03
Independence track
Ready to go independent? I help advisors navigate IBD selection, RIA platforms, and custodian relationships — so you can make the move with confidence and land in the right place.
