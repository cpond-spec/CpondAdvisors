About Corey Pond

Not transactional.
Not ever.

I work with advisors the same way the best advisors work with their clients — before, during, and long after the decision is made.

My background

I have spent years working with financial advisors across every firm type, every channel, and every stage of their career — wirehouses, regional broker-dealers, independent BDs, RIA platforms, and everything in between. Solo advisors managing $40M. Teams managing $2B. Advisors just hitting their stride. Advisors thinking about their last move.

What I have learned working across that landscape is that no two situations are the same. The advisor at a wirehouse who has been there 22 years has a completely different set of considerations than the advisor at an IBD who is ready to launch their own RIA. What they share is the same fundamental need — someone who will sit with them, understand their specific situation, and help them weigh out what their next step actually looks like for their business.

That is what I do. I help advisors understand their options, evaluate their real choices, and make the move that is right for them — not the fastest move, not the most common move, but the right one. And I stay with them through every step of the process.

01
Before the decision
I help advisors understand what is actually available to them — real options, real economics, real deal structures. Most advisors are surprised by what they find out when they finally have an honest conversation.

During the transition
From the moment a decision is made through the execution — I manage the complexity, protect your client relationships, and make sure every detail is handled correctly. You focus on your clients. I handle the rest.

After the move
My job is not done when you sign the paperwork. The first 90 days at a new firm are critical. I stay involved to make sure the transition lands the way it was planned — for you and for your clients.
